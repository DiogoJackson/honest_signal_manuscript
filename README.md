## Abstract

In fiddler crabs, males have one enlarged claw used to fight rivals and attract females. When this claw is lost, it can regenerate, but the new claw, although similar in size, is weaker and lighter. In this study, we investigated how females distinguish males with original claws from those with regenerated claws. We found that original claws are stronger and more robust, giving males an advantage during competition. Females also preferred males with original claws, regardless of claw size or color. Our findings suggest that claw shape provides reliable information about male quality, allowing females to distinguish honest signals from potentially deceptive ones. This study highlights how visual traits can help maintain honest communication in animals.

## Data Collection

Data on claw morphology and visual signals (grip strength, color, size, shape, and mass) were collected from April to July 2022. Data on male–male competition were collected from August to October 2022, while data on female mate choice were collected from July to November 2023.

All data were collected during low tide, between 09:00 and 15:00, in Barra do Rio, Extremoz, Rio Grande do Norte, Brazil.

# Repository Contents

This repository contains the datasets, morphometric information, R scripts, and output directories associated with the study.

## Main Directories

### 📁 data

Contains raw and processed datasets used in the study.

- **raw:** Original experimental data, reflectance measurements, and supporting files.
- **processed:** Datasets generated after cleaning and organizing the raw data.

### 📁 morphometry

Contains files used in the geometric morphometric analysis of fiddler crab claws.

- **amostras:** Photographs of the claws.
- **coordenadas:** Landmark coordinates extracted from the claw photographs.
- **landmarks.txt:** Names of the anatomical landmarks.

### 📁 outputs

Contains the figures and tables generated by the analysis scripts.

- **figures:** Figures used in the manuscript.
- **tables:** Statistical results and summary tables.

These directories are initially empty and are populated when the relevant scripts are executed.

### 📁 scripts

Contains R scripts used to:

- Import and clean raw data.
- Process reflectance measurements.
- Perform visual modeling.
- Analyze claw strength, mass, size, and shape.
- Analyze male–male competition.
- Analyze female mate choice.
- Generate statistical results, figures, and tables.

# Folder Structure and Data Description

## 📁 data/raw

Contains the original datasets and supporting files.

### 📁 photo_res

Photograph of a male thin-fingered fiddler crab (*Leptuca leptodactyla*).

### 📁 refletancias

Contains claw reflectance spectra obtained from field and laboratory measurements.

### 🟩 dados_mestrado_bia.csv

Raw dataset containing measurements and experimental information associated with the female mate choice experiment.

**Variables:**

- **ID:** Identification of the crabs.
- **s:** Quantum catch of the short-wavelength-sensitive photoreceptor.
- **m:** Quantum catch of the medium-wavelength-sensitive photoreceptor.
- **luminance:** Estimated luminance perceived by the crab's visual system.
- **chromatic_contrast:** Chromatic contrast between the claw and the natural mudflat background.
- **achromatic_contrast:** Achromatic contrast between the claw and the natural mudflat background.
- **vismodel:** Visual model used to estimate color perception.
- **S8:** Claw color saturation.
- **B2:** Mean claw brightness (average reflectance from 300 to 700 nm).
- **H1:** Claw hue, expressed as the wavelength of maximum reflectance (nm).
- **male_type:** Classification of the male.
- **claw_size:** Length of the male's enlarged claw (mm).
- **carapaca_size_m:** Male carapace size (mm).
- **carapaca_size_f:** Female carapace size (mm).
- **data:** Date of the experiment.
- **fugas:** Number of escape events.
- **fuga_total:** Total number of escape events.
- **female_pref:** Male preference status based on female choice.
- **claw_type:** Claw type (brachychelous/original or leptochelous/regenerated).
- **female_choice:** Female mate choice outcome (1 = chosen; 0 = not chosen).

### 🟩 data_fight.xlsx

Raw dataset containing behavioral observations from the male–male competition experiment, including burrow acquisition and competitive interactions.

**Variables:**

- **ID:** Identification of the male crab.
- **tempo_s:** Time spent during the experiment (seconds).
- **toca:** Burrow acquisition outcome (1 = acquired a burrow; 0 = did not acquire a burrow).
- **tipo_quela:** Claw type (brac = brachychelous/original; lep = leptochelous/regenerated).
- **aquisicao_de_toca:** Burrow acquisition method (combate = direct combat; roubo = burrow theft; toca_vazia = occupation of an empty burrow; sem_toca = no burrow acquired).

### 🟩 data_force.xlsx

Dataset containing claw grip strength measurements, claw mass, and autotomy records from fiddler crabs (*Leptuca leptodactyla*), including autotomy occurrence, latency, and experimental conditions.

**Variables:**

- **autotomy:** Indicates whether the crab autotomized (shed) its claw during the experiment (yes = autotomy occurred; no = autotomy did not occur).
- **autotomy_latency:** Time elapsed until claw autotomy during the experiment.
- **autotomy_trial:** Number of trials recorded in the autotomy experiment.
- **autotomy_exp_type:** Type of autotomy experiment (by_time = time-based experiment; by_trial = trial-based experiment).
- **force1:** Grip strength recorded during the first measurement (N).
- **force2:** Grip strength recorded during the second measurement (N).
- **force3:** Grip strength recorded during the third measurement (N).
- **max_force:** Maximum grip strength recorded across the three measurements (N).
- **weight_mg:** Claw mass, with values corresponding to grams (g).
- **weight_g:** Claw mass, with values corresponding to milligrams (mg).
- **data:** Date of data collection.

### 🟩 pfowl_transmittance.csv

Dataset containing light transmittance measurements from peafowl (*Pavo cristatus*).

**Variables:**

- **wl:** Wavelength of light (nm).
- **Transmission:** Proportion of light transmitted at each wavelength.
